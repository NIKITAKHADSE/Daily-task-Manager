GOOGLE SHEET CONNECTION - SIMPLE GUIDE
======================================

Your existing daily Google Sheet can stay exactly as it is.

STEP 1
Open the exact Google Sheet tab you want the dashboard to read.

STEP 2
Click Share -> General access -> Anyone with the link -> Viewer.

STEP 3
Copy the normal browser link. Example:
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/XXXXXXXXXXXX/edit?gid=123456#gid=123456

STEP 4
Start the project and login:
http://localhost:8000

Email: admin@example.com
Password: Admin123!

STEP 5
Click "Google Sheet" in the left menu.

STEP 6
Paste your Google Sheet link.

STEP 7
Set the Sheet Year.
Example: if your rows say "19 August" and the work is from 2026, enter 2026.

STEP 8
Choose Auto Sync and tick "Turn on automatic sync".

STEP 9
Click "Test Connection".
You should see how many task rows were found.

STEP 10
Click "Save Connection" and then "Sync Now".

DONE.

The application will use:
- Responsible editor = Employee
- Type = Category
- Name = Client
- Tasks = Task Description
- Remarks filled by editors = Status
- Priority = Priority

When a new Responsible editor appears in the Sheet, the app automatically creates that employee for analytics.

When a new Type appears, the app automatically creates that task category.

The Sheet remains the source of truth. If you change a Google Sheet task inside the web app, your next Sheet sync would overwrite it, so Sheet tasks are intentionally read-only in the app.
